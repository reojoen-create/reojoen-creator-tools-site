REO JOEN Creator Tools is an internal software tool operated solely by the owner of the REO JOEN YouTube channel. This policy explains how the API Client accesses, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when using YouTube API Services.

Use of YouTube API Services

The application uses YouTube API Services for owner-authorized video upload, video management, channel access, and performance measurement. Use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Information accessed or processed

Depending on the authorized scope and active workflow, the application may access or process information associated with the authorized YouTube account, including channel identity and metadata, uploaded video identifiers and metadata, video privacy status, upload results, and channel/video analytics data needed to operate and measure the owner's content workflow.

The application does not request, collect, or store the user's Google or YouTube password.

How information is used

Authorized data is used only to operate, validate, secure, and measure the owner's YouTube content workflow, including private-first upload verification, owner-authorized video management, duplicate prevention, quality-control evidence, and analytics reporting.

Storage and security

OAuth client secrets and refresh tokens are stored only in protected secret storage used by the automation environment and are not published on this website, written to project documentation, or committed to source control. Operational records may retain non-secret evidence such as video IDs, timestamps, privacy status, workflow/run identifiers, hashes, and quality-control results.

Sharing and sale of data

The application does not sell authorized YouTube data or personal information. It does not provide authorized YouTube data to advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers acting on behalf of the owner only as necessary to run the authorized workflow, subject to their applicable security and service terms, or when disclosure is required by law.

Advertising, cookies, and device storage

This informational website does not serve third-party advertising and does not intentionally use advertising cookies. The internal API Client is not offered as a public consumer service.

Authorization, revocation, and deletion

The authorized user may revoke the application's Google/YouTube access through Google Account permissions at Google Account — Third-party access. The owner may also discontinue the API Client and remove its stored authorized data.

After authorization is revoked, Authorized Data obtained under that authorization will be deleted as soon as practicable and no later than 7 calendar days, except information that must be retained to comply with applicable law. Revoked OAuth credentials will no longer be used.

Data retention

Authorized API Data is retained only for as long as necessary for the permitted operational purpose and applicable YouTube API policies. Where a YouTube API policy imposes a shorter retention or refresh requirement, that requirement controls. Non-secret operational evidence that is not Authorized Data may be retained for audit, security, and reliability purposes.

User control

The channel owner retains final authority over actions taken on the owner's behalf. Publication-related actions are designed to remain controlled and fail-closed until applicable authorization, policy, and quality gates are satisfied.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the API Client's functionality, applicable law, or YouTube/Google API requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For questions, complaints, or deletion requests related to this privacy policy or the API Client's data practices, contact reojoen@gmail.com.
